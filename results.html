Scoring Information

All contests in the F3L Contest Series use the F3XVault scoring system for accurate, real-time results and standings.

How Series Points Work

The 2026 series uses standard scoring based on event percentage:

  • Points are calculated as a percentage of the winner's score
  • Your best three events count toward your final series score
  • The number of events that count increases as additional events are added to the series
