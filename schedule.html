Registration Information

Registration details for each contest will be posted approximately 8-10 weeks before the event date. Watch this space and join our mailing list for notifications.

What to Expect

  • Two-day contest format with multiple rounds. Friday practice sessions (where available)
  • FAI F3L rules apply. Each contest will have its own Contest Bulletin to highlight any field-specific rule changes and event schedule
  • Electronic timing and scoring using F3XVault
  • Awards to top competitors within each event and annual awards to the top pilots.
